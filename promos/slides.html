The catalog

A source is
three answers

Which elements, which of them count, and what Recombee is told about each.

  • Entries, products, categories, assets, users
  • Twenty built-in mappers, Twig for anything else
  • A content fingerprint, so an unchanged re-sync is free
  • Saves are batched into one queue job per request

Product  ·  TP Products

Which of them count
Live elements only
Disabled, expired and pending elements are removed from Recombee rather than quietly left behind to be recommended.

price  ·  onSale  ·  inStock  ·  categories

Interactions

The funnel,
recorded

A recommender is only as good as what it has watched people do.

  • Identity is resolved server-side, never off the request
  • Commerce carts and completed orders are wired up for you
  • Consent is read from a cookie before anything is sent
  • Guests are merged into the account they sign into

In your templates

Four calls,
real elements

Every one hands back Craft elements, not IDs you have to go and look up.

  • Short timeout, no retries — a page render never waits
  • Fails open — an empty set, a logged reason, no 500
  • Filters to live elements on the Recombee side, not after
  • Scenarios let one database serve every slot on the site

Operations

Provable
in a deploy

Eleven checks, the same ones the control panel runs, exiting non-zero so a pipeline can stop on them.

  • Credentials, region, connection and round-trip time
  • Every declared property, checked for type drift
  • A connection log with payloads and never a credential
  • A dry-run switch that logs requests without sending
