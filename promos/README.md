# Plugin Store promo images

Marketing images for the Bee listing on the Craft Plugin Store, rendered in the same theme as the
plugin's marketing page at
[justinholt.com/plugins/craft-bee](https://justinholt.com/plugins/craft-bee).

They live in the plugin repo, not beside a site, because Bee is one of the plugins that lives as a
page on justinholt.com rather than on its own domain. The plugins with their own domain keep their
deck in their `craft-*-website` repo instead.

## Building

```bash
./build.sh          # all slides
./build.sh "2 5"    # just slides 2 and 5
```

Output lands in `out/` as `bee-promo-N.png`, 1920×1080 (rendered at 2× in headless Chrome, then
downsampled so the type stays crisp).

## Notes

`assets/icon.svg` **is** a straight copy of `../src/icon.svg`, unlike Bouncer's. Bee's icon is
hand-authored rather than traced: the tile is a real `<rect>` and the mark is a group of closed
paths, so there is no full-canvas white frame path to strip and no transparent padding to crop out.
Copy it over whole when the icon changes.

`assets/watermark.svg` is `../src/icon-mask.svg` with `fill="currentColor"` switched to `#ffffff`.
**Three files, one geometry** — change the icon and change all three.

**The watermark needed to go well below the family's usual opacity.** Bee's mark is the opposite of
Bouncer's dense figure: wide, airy, four wings and three gaps across the abdomen. That makes it
survive a much larger crop — 1080px at `left: -17%`, where Bouncer needs 980px at `-20%` — but it
also makes it *lighter*, and at the family's filled-mark 0.020 it read as a recognisable bee parked
behind the headline on every slide. **0.013** was better, and still legible on the centred slides
where the left half of the canvas is empty; **0.009** puts it under the threshold.

Two layout rules inherited from the rest of the family, both of which bit again:

- **One line per bullet, about 55 characters at 25px.** The copy column is 780px inside a 130px
  gutter and the panel starts at `left: 900px`, so a bullet that wraps runs at the panel and lands
  on top of it — nothing clips, the text simply overlaps. "Fails open: an empty set, a logged
  reason, a page that renders" is 61 characters and did exactly that.
- **`.points li` is a flex container.** An inline `<span class="mono">` left loose in the text
  becomes a *sibling flex item* and picks up the 16px gap on both sides. Every point keeps its text
  in one `<span class="t">`.

**`.place .chip` drops the family's `text-transform: uppercase`.** Everywhere else in the family a
chip carries a label; on slide 5 it carries a Twig call, and `TOUSER` is not a thing anyone can
type. The calls are also the *Twig* names — `recommend`, `related`, `segment`, `search` — not the
service methods behind them (`toUser`, `toItem`, `toItemSegment`, `search`), which is the pair most
likely to get mixed up when this copy is edited.

Every screenshot in `shots/` needs `.cpc-clip`: Bee's control panel screens are all long tables, and
at the 950px the panel gives them the catalog capture alone comes out well past the 620px cap. The
fade makes the cut read as "there is more" rather than as a broken edge.

`fonts.css` is generated by `build.sh`, which base64-inlines the two woff2 files in `assets/` —
Chrome's `file://` origin rules block them otherwise. It is gitignored; don't edit it.

## Where the screenshots come from

`shots/` is copied from `plugin-shots/out/bee/`. They are real captures of a real install, not
mockups: `~/Sites/plugin-testing` with Bee connected, 169 items synced and the order history
backfilled. See `../tests/shots/README.md` for how that install is driven without a live Recombee
database.
